# Built-in icon attribution

The marks below are trademarks of their owners. They are shipped so a plugin can link to the service each one
names, which is the use their brand guidelines describe. Each PNG is a white (or white and brand colored) mark on a
transparent background, rasterized at 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64 and 128 pixels from a simplified 24 unit outline.

| Icon | Files | Owner | Guidelines |
|---|---|---|---|
| `NoireIcon.Discord` | `discord_*.png` | Discord Inc. | https://discord.com/branding |
| `NoireIcon.Kofi` | `kofi_*.png` | Ko-fi Labs Limited | https://more.ko-fi.com/brand-assets |

Do not add a mark whose owner does not allow redistribution for linking; register it at runtime with
`NoireIcons.Register` from the consuming plugin instead.
